Give me a random number between zero and ten.

Let answer be that.
Let guess be one thousand.
Let guesses be zero.

Repeat
    Ask for a number.
    Let guess be that.
    If guess is greater than ten then
        Say "Your guess must be in the range zero to ten".
    Otherwise
        If answer is less than guess then
            Say "Lower!".
        Otherwise if answer is greater than guess then
            Say "Higher!".
        Let guesses be guesses plus one.
Until guess is answer or guesses is three.

If guess is answer then
    Say "Well done! You guessed the number".
Otherwise
    Say "Better luck next time!".

Goodbye.
