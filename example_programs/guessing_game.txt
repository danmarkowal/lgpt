Give me a random number between zero and one hundred.

Remember that answer is that.
Remember that guess is one thousand.
Remember that guesses is zero.

Repeat
    Ask for a number.
    If that is greater than one hundred then
        Say "your guess must be in the range zero to one hundred".
    Otherwise
        Remember that guess is that.
        Remember that guesses is guesses plus one.
until guess is answer or guesses is three.

If guess is answer then
    Say "Well done! You guessed the number".
Otherwise
    Say "Better luck next time!".

Goodbye.
