Give me a random number between zero and ten.

Let answer be that.
Let guess be one thousand.
Let guesses be zero.

Repeat
    Ask for a number.
    If that is greater than one hundred then
        Say "your guess must be in the range zero to one hundred".
    otherwise
        Remember that guess is that.
        Remember that guesses is guesses plus one.
until guess is answer or guesses is three.

If guess is answer then
    Say line "Well done! You guessed the number".
otherwise
    Say line "Better luck next time!".

Goodbye.
